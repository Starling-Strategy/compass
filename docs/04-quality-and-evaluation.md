# 4. Quality & Evaluation

> **Stub — to be drafted.** This doc explains how the team knows Compass is working
> and how failures turn into fixes.

Planned contents:

- The three quality layers, distinguished up front: (a) answer-time verification
  built into the pipeline (see [Product & Answer Flow](02-product-and-answer-flow.md)),
  (b) benchmark evaluation before and between releases (this doc), (c) post-launch
  monitoring and staff feedback.
- The seven accuracy dimensions — selection, data fidelity, coverage-state labeling,
  filtering, sorting, citations, consistency — plus voice & tone, and what each means.
- The scenario library: scenarios, cases, and criteria (326 active scenarios at last
  audit), how they define correct behavior, and per-dimension launch thresholds.
- How accuracy is measured concretely, with a dated evaluation-results artifact
  compiled from the evaluation runs.
- Data-quality checks on the pipeline side: validation gates, audits, and reporting.
